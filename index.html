job-funnel

job-funnel is a personal job-search tracker. It runs on its owner's own computer and reads their own job-search mail, read-only, to keep track of the applications they have sent and the replies they get: acknowledgements, rejections, interview invitations and recruiters' job descriptions.

It is used by one person, its developer, for their own Gmail account. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

  • It asks Google only for read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • It reads only job-related messages; other mail is skipped by its sender and subject without being opened.
  • What it reads stays in the owner's private database. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
